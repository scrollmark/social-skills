## Prompt

I made a casual TikTok (45 seconds, talking to camera) about how I accidentally discovered that working in 25-minute sprints with 5-minute breaks tripled my output. It got 200K views. How do I repurpose this for LinkedIn?

## Without skill (baseline)

Claude suggests posting the same video on LinkedIn, maybe with a longer caption. Or suggests writing a post that basically transcribes the TikTok script. Doesn't meaningfully adapt for the platform.

## With skill (expected)

Claude treats this as adaptation, not copying. Identifies what stays (the core insight about sprint-based work) and what changes (everything about the delivery). The LinkedIn version should: use a hook in the first 2-3 lines that frames the insight professionally ("I tripled my output last quarter. Here's the system."), tell the story with professional context (what kind of work, what the impact was), use LinkedIn's text formatting conventions (line breaks, short paragraphs), avoid TikTok-native language, and probably not include the video at all — or reframe it. May suggest putting any link in comments, not the post body.

## Behavioral markers

- [ ] Explicitly separates what stays (core insight) from what changes (format, framing, tone)
- [ ] Does NOT suggest posting the same video on LinkedIn
- [ ] Suggests a LinkedIn-native hook for the first 2-3 lines
- [ ] Adds professional framing to the personal story
- [ ] Mentions at least one LinkedIn-specific convention (link in comments, line breaks, "see more" fold)
