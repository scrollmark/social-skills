## Prompt

Someone left this comment on a fitness influencer's gym selfie post: "oh wow another gym selfie how original 🙄"

What's the sentiment here? Who is this person likely to be?

## Without skill (baseline)

Claude reads the comment as mildly negative or critical. May note the eye-roll emoji but treat the comment as straightforward criticism. Likely misses the performative aspect.

## With skill (expected)

Claude identifies the ironic register before interpreting. Notes the 🙄 as confirmation of dismissiveness (not ambiguous). Identifies this as a non-target-audience interaction — likely someone who was algorithmically served the content rather than a follower. Frames this as noise rather than actionable feedback.

## Behavioral markers

- [ ] Explicitly identifies the register (ironic/sarcastic) before interpretation
- [ ] References the 🙄 emoji as a meaning-modifier, not just decoration
- [ ] Distinguishes between target audience and non-target audience
- [ ] Does not recommend the creator "take this feedback seriously"
