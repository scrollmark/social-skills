## Prompt

Under a dance video, someone commented: "I just watched this 47 times 💀😭"

Is this a positive or negative comment?

## Without skill (baseline)

Claude may be uncertain. The skull and crying emoji could read as negative (death, sadness). Claude might hedge or say "mixed sentiment." May note the number 47 as hyperbole but miss the overall register.

## With skill (expected)

Claude immediately identifies both emoji as positive/humor indicators in this context. 💀 = "I'm dead laughing" and 😭 = overwhelmed with how good this is. The "47 times" is hyperbolic praise — they watched it on repeat because they loved it. This is unambiguously enthusiastic praise.

## Behavioral markers

- [ ] Identifies 💀 as humor/laughing, not literal death
- [ ] Identifies 😭 as overwhelm/positive, not sadness
- [ ] Reads the overall comment as enthusiastic praise
- [ ] Does not hedge or call it "mixed" sentiment
