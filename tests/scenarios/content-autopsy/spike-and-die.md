## Prompt

My Reel got 500K views (my average is 15K) but I only gained 12 new followers from it. What happened?

## Without skill (baseline)

Claude congratulates the views, maybe suggests the content wasn't "targeted enough" or recommends adding a CTA. Doesn't systematically analyze the metrics or identify the performance pattern.

## With skill (expected)

Claude identifies this as a spike-and-die pattern: the content appealed broadly (500K views) but didn't convert to followers (12 is extremely low for that reach). Investigates possible causes: the content likely worked because of a trending format, sound, or broadly relatable topic — meaning the trend or topic did the heavy lifting, not the creator's unique angle. The viewers had no reason to follow because the content didn't represent what the creator usually makes. Notes this isn't necessarily bad — the reach has other value — but if the goal is growth, future viral attempts need a stronger connection to the creator's core content.

## Behavioral markers

- [ ] Names or describes the spike-and-die pattern
- [ ] Identifies the core issue: high reach, low conversion
- [ ] Investigates WHY (trend/topic did the work, not the creator's identity)
- [ ] Frames it as forensics, not judgment ("here's what likely happened" not "this was bad")
- [ ] Distinguishes between reach value and growth value
