## Prompt

I have a recipe video showing how to make a 5-minute pasta that looks restaurant-quality. How would I hook this for TikTok vs YouTube?

## Without skill (baseline)

Claude gives similar advice for both platforms — maybe "start with the finished dish" or "ask a question." Doesn't meaningfully differentiate between what makes a hook work on each platform.

## With skill (expected)

Claude gives distinctly different hooks for each platform, based on how hooks mechanically work on each one.

TikTok: visual-first, text overlay, first frame. E.g., "Show the finished dish in the first frame with text overlay: '5 minutes. $4. Your date will think you're a chef.' — the visual does the work, text creates the curiosity gap."

YouTube: thumbnail + title IS the hook, before the video even plays. E.g., "Thumbnail: split image — $4 grocery bag on one side, plated restaurant-looking pasta on the other. Title: 'I Made Restaurant Pasta in 5 Minutes for $4.' Then the first 30 seconds of video justify the click — show the speed, show the result."

## Behavioral markers

- [ ] Gives meaningfully different hooks for each platform (not the same hook reworded)
- [ ] TikTok hook focuses on visual/text overlay in first frame
- [ ] YouTube hook addresses thumbnail and title as the primary hook mechanism
- [ ] YouTube hook includes guidance for the first 30 seconds of video (justifying the click)
