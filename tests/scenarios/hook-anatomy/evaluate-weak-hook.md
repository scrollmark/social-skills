## Prompt

Evaluate this video opening: "Hey guys, welcome back to my channel! Today I'm going to talk about three morning habits that changed my life."

## Without skill (baseline)

Claude may say the opening is reasonable — it tells the viewer what to expect. Might suggest minor improvements like "being more specific" but treats the structure as fundamentally sound.

## With skill (expected)

Claude identifies the structural problems: no open loop (the viewer already knows exactly what's coming), "hey guys welcome back" is dead time with no hook value, and the promise isn't specific or surprising enough to create curiosity. Suggests a specific structural fix using one of the hook patterns — e.g., starting with a curiosity gap ("I stopped setting an alarm six months ago and I've never been more productive") or a pattern interrupt.

## Behavioral markers

- [ ] Identifies the lack of an open loop
- [ ] Calls out "hey guys welcome back" as an anti-pattern
- [ ] Explains WHY it's weak (no reason to stay past 3 seconds)
- [ ] Suggests a specific alternative using a named hook pattern
